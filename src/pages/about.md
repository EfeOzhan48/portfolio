### What Am I Working On?

Rather than sticking to a single field in engineering, I love getting under the hood to see how systems actually work. Driven by the curiosity of *"what’s happening behind the scenes?"*, I experiment with various technologies and explore what suits me best.

Alongside coding, staying active within developer communities is important to me:
* **TEVED (Technology and Productivity Association):** Part of the founding team where we strive to spread technology and productivity practices ([teved.org](https://teved.org)).
* **GDG Samsun:** Organizing community events and meetups with the Google Developer Groups Samsun team.
* **University Clubs:** Actively participating in student clubs and taking part in new projects.
